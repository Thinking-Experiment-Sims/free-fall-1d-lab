🦝 The Well Toss: Symmetry & Free Fall

Core Question: A raccoon tosses a stone upwards at +15 m/s into a 20 m well. How does it compare to throwing it straight down at -15 m/s?

  1. Observe the stone reach apex at t = 1.53 s (h = +11.48 m above lip).
  2. Note return to lip level at t = 3.06 s with velocity v = -15 m/s.
  3. Toggle Compare Downward Throw: both stones strike the water at the exact same speed (24.84 m/s)!
  4. Verify time difference is exactly 2 × t_apex = 3.06 s.
🧗 Cliff Drop: Simultaneous Splash

Core Question: Stone 1 is tossed up at +2.0 m/s from a 50 m cliff. Stone 2 is launched 1.0 s later. What initial velocity creates a single splash?

  1. Stone 1 requires 3.405 s to reach water level (y = 0).
  2. Stone 2 only has 2.405 s of flight time.
  3. Solve position equation: Stone 2 must be hurled downward at v02 = -9.01 m/s!
  4. Watch both stones meet at the water line at the exact same millisecond.
🚀 Two-Stage Model Rocket: Boost & Coast

Core Question: A rocket accelerates upward at +2.0 m/s² until burnout at 150 m, then enters free fall under gravity. What is apex and flight time?

  1. Phase 1: Burns for t = 2.84 s reaching burnout velocity v = 55.68 m/s.
  2. Observe the acceleration step function from +2.0 m/s² to -9.8 m/s² on the a(t) graph.
  3. Rocket coasts upward for another 5.68 s, reaching apex at 308.16 m!
  4. Total flight time to ground impact: t = 16.45 s at v = -77.72 m/s.
⚙️ Free Fall Sandbox: Multi-Body Dynamics

Core Question: How do initial launch height, speed, and planetary gravity reshape kinematic curves?

  1. Compare Earth (g = 9.8 m/s²) with Moon (1.62 m/s²) and Mars (3.71 m/s²).
  2. Test Zero-G: notice linear position graph and constant velocity line.
